# Round 9 — Final Cross-Paper Evidence Matrix

## 1. Purpose and scope

这份矩阵是 Round 9 的第一份正式产物。它不对论文做“效果排名”，也不复述论文摘要，而是统一回答：

> **What signal was optimized, what component was updated, what behavior was actually observed, what intervention isolated that behavior, and what transfer evidence is really available?**

矩阵纳入当前文献池中能够直接支持 Survey 主线的 `Core`、`Supporting`、`Evaluation` 和 `Core Mechanism` 论文。邻近 Survey、已经明确排除的普通 RAG/Memory/Tool-use 论文，以及仅用于背景说明但不进入核心论证的条目不作为能力证据计入。

### Evidence vocabulary

- **Capability Isolation:** `Strong` 需要有目标行为级指标或受控干预；`Medium` 表示有局部行为指标或组件消融；`Limited` 表示主要依赖端点结果或弱代理；`Not Tested` 表示没有能力隔离实验。
- **Deployment Parity:** `State-only` 表示训练/推理信号原则上来自部署可见状态；`Partial` 表示同时存在部署可见和 privileged signal；`Privileged` 表示依赖 Gold Answer、完整未来轨迹或 oracle evidence；`N/A` 表示评价资源而非 policy training。
- **Generalization:** 只记录实际改变的维度。新 benchmark、新 backbone、多个数据集不自动等于 Behavior Transfer。
- **Evidence Limitation:** 每行最后的判断是该论文不能支持什么，而不是对方法价值的否定。

## 3. Cross-matrix observations

### 3.1 Positive evidence is capability-specific

- **A1:** CoBRA and Calibrate-Then-Act provide the clearest local evidence that a policy can alter tool-use decisions under cost or paired no-tool/tool conditions. This is not yet calibrated, ex-ante VOI transfer.
- **A2:** QREF and StepSearch provide the closest process/coverage signals; Environment-aware IR provides the clearest negative evidence that query policies learn retriever-specific dialects.
- **A3:** MARAG-R1 and DDPO demonstrate trainable coverage/diversity behavior, but Tool Selection and ToolSearcher show why tool discovery should not be counted automatically as information-space exploration.
- **A4:** S2G-RAG and RLSeek are direct narrow post-training anchors; AuthorityBench, Sufficient Context and ConfRAG show that measurement coverage is broader than learned general evidence judgment.
- **A5:** ClashEval and Source-balancing provide the strongest output-level replacement evidence; BRIEF, CARE and TRACE-the-Evidence provide indirect composition evidence; process-level causal reliance remains unestablished.
- **A6:** AnySearch is strongest for unseen-budget behavior; AutoSearch for stopping depth; CriticSearch, CW-GRPO, TRACE-turn and SSPO improve credit/supervision but do not complete the fixed-history feedback test.

### 3.2 The evidence vector is not a single maturity score

A paper can be strong on `Method Effectiveness` and weak on `Capability Isolation`; it can have `Task OOD` while having no `Behavior Transfer`; it can have a direct behavior label while using privileged training information. The matrix therefore keeps the following dimensions separate:

```text
Training–Capability Alignment
Capability Isolation
Deployment Information Parity
Generalization Regime
Behavior Transfer
```

### 3.3 The most common evidence boundary

Across the matrix, the strongest reproducible claim is usually:

> **The method changes an observable behavior or endpoint under a particular signal and information environment.**

The stronger claims—general capability acquisition, causal reliance on the intended information, online state-conditioned control, and transfer to a new information environment—require separate evidence and should not be inferred from the endpoint.

### 3.4 Round 9 traceability rule

The next Claim–Evidence Matrix must cite these row IDs rather than whole papers vaguely. A paper may play different roles for different claims: for example, `TRACE-turn` supports the existence of useful state-value credit but also supports the Hindsight–Deployment Gap; `AnySearch` is counterevidence to the claim that all budget behavior is fixed-template, while still being insufficient evidence for Retriever/Tool Transfer.
